## Where the closure pass pushed back

- **Item 1's framing**: the "confirmed-input layer" and rebuild/rescore
  mechanism was not missing — it is the tested PF-2-lineage pipeline. The
  genuine gap was that the §8 sitting only measured. Recorded so the fix is
  understood as an extension, not a rebuild.
- **Measurement vs closure**: applying every ruling would make post-closure
  "precision" 100% by construction. The machine-error measurement is therefore
  always computed from FIRST-pass rulings, before reconciliation and
  application, and the amendment says so. The §8 floor still gates families on
  measured machine precision — an instrument that was wrong about a family too
  often does not get to publish that family's Tier L numbers merely because
  every error was later hand-fixed.
- **"Remove that promise"** (item 5's alternative): the cross-check was
  implemented instead. A protocol promise in a frozen amendment is kept or
  amended in the open, not quietly dropped.
