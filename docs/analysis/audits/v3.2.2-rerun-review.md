## The raw findings, as returned (verbatim summaries; abridged only of file-path noise)

**1. `apply-review-rulings.mjs` — a ruling on an R17-merged proposition lands
on only ONE of its records. CONFIRMED (executed).** `mergeDuplicates` sets the
ledger row's `charStart` to the min of its constituents, so the packet key row
carries the lowest charStart; the disambiguator then narrows to the single
record with that charStart, contradicting the comment above it. Executed: a
BAD ruling withdrew only constituent A — the false row was rebuilt from B and
remained in the ledger, unruled; a CORRECTED ruling corrected only A, the
records stopped sharing MERGE_FIELDS, and one false statement became two.

**2. Matching used the confirmed record's RAW kind while the key row carried
the ledger row's POST-correction kind; a ruling could be stamped on a
different claim. CONFIRMED (executed).** With record A (`not_mafia_claim`,
`corrected.kind: role_claim`) and record B (`role_claim`) at one seq, A's key
row matched only B — A's BAD withdrew an unrelated valid false statement and A
survived unadjudicated.

**3. Newly created false rows arrived pre-stamped, so S10 declared closure
over rows never adjudicated as false. CONFIRMED.** Every row the apply touched
or created carried a `reviewRuling`; S10 checked only that the stamp existed.
A message-scan recovery that scored false, or a true-sample row whose
CORRECTED fields flipped it false, satisfied S10 on arrival — the file's own
header asserted the opposite invariant.

**4. `kind: '-'` struck the kind, bypassed the published-family check, and
passed the R13 effective-proposition check vacuously. CONFIRMED (executed).**
The strike deleted `effective.kind`, `KIND_REQUIRED[undefined] ?? []` had no
missing fields, and the next stage (build-ledger) died on UNSCORABLE instead.

**5. The publication's lineage check was a tautology, and the applied rulings
were never bound to the published evidence. CONFIRMED.** Both compared values
were read from the same key-file bytes; `rulingsSha256` had one producer and
zero consumers — a summary computed from `ryan-rulings.json` could publish
beside a ledger built from `ryan-final.json`.

**6. The entire §8 omission audit in G5 was nested under
`secondHumanRater !== true`; a manifest rater-count change switched it off.
CONFIRMED.**

**7. The §8 floors were hardcoded in G5 and the Wilson formula existed as a
third copy. CONFIRMED.** A frozen-parameter change would have produced a fake
data-corruption failure.

**8. `EVALUATOR_VERSION` was left at v3.2.1 across a behavioral scorer change
(CORRECTABLE_FIELDS + merge semantics). CONFIRMED (git).**

**9. A struck recovered night vanished with no marker and no note, against
§2's "countable, never invisible". CONFIRMED (executed).**

**10. A final ruling could be stamped with the first-pass rule that justified
the OPPOSITE outcome. CONFIRMED.** `ruleFor` fell back to the first-pass rule;
nothing required an overriding final to cite its own.

**Answers to the two explicit questions:** yes on both — findings 1–2 gave two

unadjudicated false rows passing S10.
